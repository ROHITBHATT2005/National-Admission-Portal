About the Counselling Process

The National Admission Portal conducts a centralized, transparent, and fully digital counselling process for admissions to top institutes across India. The system ensures that every seat is allocated purely on the basis of merit (rank/score) and the preferences filled by the candidate.

Our counselling process eliminates manual errors, reduces paperwork, and provides equal opportunities to all students, regardless of their background.

Key Highlights of Our Counselling

  • Fully Online Process – Right from registration to seat acceptance, everything is done online.
  • Merit-Based Allocation – Seats are allocated strictly based on candidate rank and preferences.
  • Transparent Algorithm – The seat allotment algorithm is open, auditable, and fair.
  • Multiple Rounds – Candidates get multiple opportunities through Round 1, Round 2, and Spot Counselling.
  • Upgradation Option – Accept a seat and still be considered for higher preferences in subsequent rounds.
  • Real-Time Updates – Instant notifications on allotment results, cutoffs, and vacancies.
  • Centralized Document Verification – Upload documents once; verified digitally across all institutes.
  • Helpline Support – Dedicated helpline for technical and process-related queries.

Step-by-Step Counselling Process

  1. Registration: Create an account using your basic details. Receive a unique application number.
  2. Document Upload: Upload scanned copies of required certificates (marksheets, category, etc.).
  3. Choice Filling: Select your preferred institutes and programs in order of priority. You can reorder choices anytime before locking.
  4. Choice Locking: Lock your preferences before the deadline. Unlocked choices will not be considered.
  5. Mock Allotment (Optional): A trial run to help you understand the allotment logic and adjust preferences.
  6. Round 1 Allotment: Based on merit and locked choices, seats are allocated. Results published on the portal.
  7. Acceptance & Upgradation: After Round 1, you can accept the seat, accept & upgrade (participate in next round), or reject.
  8. Round 2 & Further Rounds: Additional rounds for vacant seats, with upgradation options.
  9. Spot Counselling: Final round to fill remaining vacancies after all regular rounds.
  10. Fee Payment & Reporting: Pay seat acceptance fee and report to the allotted institute with original documents.

⚖️ Commitment to Fairness & Transparency

Our counselling system is designed to be completely bias-free. Key features that ensure fairness:

  • Seat allocation algorithm is based on published merit lists (entrance exam ranks / board percentages).
  • All category-wise seat matrices are displayed publicly before choice filling.
  • Real-time cutoff ranks are released after each round.
  • Grievance redressal mechanism – students can raise objections online.
  • Audit trail maintained for every action (login, choice change, allotment).

Who Can Participate?

Candidates who have appeared for the relevant national/state entrance examination or meet the eligibility criteria of participating institutes can register. Reservation policies (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD) as per Government of India norms are applicable.

Eligibility details for each institute and program are available on their respective pages.
