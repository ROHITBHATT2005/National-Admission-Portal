About the Portal

The National Admission Portal brings all participating institutes and programs under one unified digital platform. Students can register, fill choices, check cutoffs, and secure seats online — eliminating paperwork and delays.

✔ Fully automated seat allocation based on merit & preferences
✔ Real-time updates on allotment, cutoffs, and vacancies
✔ Secure document verification and digital locking

Why Choose This Portal?

✔ No manual errors or middlemen
✔ Instant seat allotment results
✔ Access to institute-wise seats, previous year cutoffs
✔ Designed for students with 24/7 helpline support
