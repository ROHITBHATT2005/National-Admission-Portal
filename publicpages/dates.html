Below are the important dates for the National Admission Portal counselling process. All timings are as per Indian Standard Time (IST).

Event Date Status

Important Notes

  • All dates are tentative and subject to change. Candidates are advised to check the portal regularly for updates.
  • Registration and choice filling will close strictly at the mentioned deadlines. No extensions will be granted.
  • Locked choices are final. Unlocked choices will not be considered for allotment.
  • Seat allotment result will be published on the student dashboard and via SMS/email.
  • Failure to pay the seat acceptance fee within the deadline will lead to cancellation of the allotted seat.
  • Original documents must be carried at the time of reporting to the institute.
