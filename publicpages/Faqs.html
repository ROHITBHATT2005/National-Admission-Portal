Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Find answers to common queries about the National Admission Portal, counselling process, seat allotment, and more.

📝 Registration & Login

1. How do I register for counselling?
Visit the portal homepage and click on "Student Login". If you are a new user, click on "New Registration". Fill in your personal details, upload documents, and pay the registration fee (if applicable). After successful registration, you will receive an application number.
2. I forgot my password. How can I reset it?
On the login page, click "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email ID or mobile number. You will receive an OTP to reset your password. Follow the instructions to create a new password.
3. What is the application number and where do I find it?
The application number is a unique 10-digit number generated after successful registration. It is sent to your registered email and mobile. You will need it for all future logins and communications.

🎯 Choice Filling & Locking

4. When does choice filling start?
Choice filling for Round 1 begins on May 20, 2026 and closes on June 20, 2026. You can add, remove, or reorder preferences any number of times before locking.
5. Is it mandatory to lock my choices?
Yes, you must lock your choices before the deadline. Unlocked choices will not be considered for seat allotment. After locking, no further changes are allowed.
6. Can I change my choices after locking?
No, once locked, choices become final. However, during the "upgradation" window after Round 1, you may modify choices for subsequent rounds if you choose not to accept the allotted seat.

🏅 Seat Allotment

7. How is seat allotment done?
Seats are allocated purely on merit (rank/score) and the order of preferences filled by the candidate. The system runs an automated algorithm that ensures fairness and transparency.
8. When will the Round 1 allotment results be announced?
Round 1 results are scheduled for July 5, 2026. You can check your dashboard or the allotment status card on the homepage.
9. What are my options after receiving a seat allotment?
You have three options: Accept (freeze the seat), Accept & Upgrade (hold the seat but participate in next round for a better preference), or Reject (forfeit the seat and exit counselling).

📄 Document Verification

10. What documents are required for verification?
Common documents: Class 10 & 12 mark sheets, category certificate (if applicable), Domicile certificate, PwD certificate, and a recent photograph. Exact list is available on the portal.
11. Is the verification online or offline?
Initial document verification is completely online. You need to upload scanned copies. Selected candidates may be required to produce original documents at the time of reporting to the institute.

💰 Fee Payment

12. What is the seat acceptance fee?
The seat acceptance fee is ₹15,000 (refundable for those who do not take admission). It is to be paid after allotment to confirm your seat.
13. When is the last date to pay the fee?
Fee payment must be completed by July 20, 2026. Failure to pay on time will result in forfeiture of the allotted seat.

❓ General Questions

14. Is there a helpline number for technical support?
Yes, you can call our helpline at 1800-XXX-2026 (toll-free) from 10 AM to 8 PM on weekdays. You can also email support@nap.edu.in.
15. Can I apply for multiple programs in the same institute?
Yes, you can add multiple programs from the same institute as separate preferences. The allotment algorithm will consider each preference independently.
16. What is the mock allotment?
Mock allotment is a trial run conducted before actual allotment. It helps candidates understand the process and adjust their preferences. Mock allotment is scheduled for June 25, 2026.
17. Are there any reservations for NRI/OCI candidates?
Yes, 12 institutes offer supernumerary seats for NRI/OCI candidates. Check the seat matrix and institute-specific guidelines for details.

🏫 After Allotment & Reporting

18. What is the reporting deadline after seat allotment?
Candidates must report to the allotted institute by July 20, 2026 with original documents for final verification. Late reporting may lead to cancellation.
19. Can I withdraw my seat after acceptance?
Yes, you can withdraw before the final admission deadline. Withdrawal rules and refund policies are available in the information brochure.
20. What happens if I do not get any seat in Round 1?
You will automatically be considered for Round 2 and subsequent rounds if you have not been allotted a seat. Also, spot counselling rounds will be held for vacant seats.
